RUM for Small Business 2026

If your site does under 100,000 sessions per month, you can run production RUM for free or for under $100/month. Here's the free-vs-paid comparison.

Direct answer
What's the cheapest viable RUM setup for a small business site?
Cloudflare Web Analytics is free with no cap if you only need Core Web Vitals. For errors + replay, Sentry's free tier (5K errors + 50 replays/month) is sufficient for early commercial use. For commercial workloads under 100K sessions/month with no replay needed, Raygun Basic at $80/month billed annually covers a flat 100K-session allowance.

The free options

  • Cloudflare Web Analytics — free, unlimited, no published cap. Core Web Vitals only. No replay, no errors, no custom events. Best fit when Cloudflare proxies the site.
  • Sentry free tier — 5,000 errors + 50 replays + 10,000 transactions per month. Works for hobby and pre-launch.
  • Fullstory free tier — 30,000 sessions/month with 12-month retention. Most generous free tier in the matrix.
  • Grafana Faro self-hosted — open-source, zero SaaS cost, infrastructure cost only.
  • Raygun free trial — 5,000 sessions/month, time-limited.

The cheapest paid tiers

  • Sematext Experience Basic — $9/month for 5,000 sessions, 7-day retention.
  • Raygun Basic — $80/month billed annually ($120/month monthly) for 100,000 sessions/month, 60-day retention, no replay.
  • Sentry Team — $26/month annual ($312/year) including modest replay allowance.
  • Atatus Startup — $49/month for 100,000 page views with APM + RUM + Logs bundled.
  • LogRocket Core — $69/month for 10,000 sessions including pixel-perfect replay.

Decision flow by need

  • Only need Core Web Vitals? Cloudflare Web Analytics, free.
  • Need errors + a bit of replay for debugging? Sentry free tier or Team plan.
  • Need replay-first product? Fullstory free 30K (if within limit) or LogRocket Core $69/mo.
  • Need flat-rate commercial RUM without replay? Raygun Basic $80/month billed annually.
  • Need bundled APM + RUM at small scale? Atatus Startup $49/month.

What to avoid for small business

  • Datadog RUM — no free tier and per-session pricing scales aggressively. Below 1M sessions/month, the cost isn't justified by feature breadth advantage.
  • Catchpoint — $10K/yr floor is enterprise pricing.
  • Akamai mPulse — quote-only, enterprise-only.
  • Splunk RUM — enterprise-bundled inside Observability Cloud.
