RUM Cost for 100K Pageviews/Month 2026

At 100,000 monthly page views (roughly 50,000 sessions), most free tiers fit comfortably and the cheapest commercial RUM is under $10/month effective. Datadog and Dynatrace are overkill at this volume.

Direct answer
What's the cheapest RUM for 100K pageviews?
Cloudflare Web Analytics is free and Sentry's free tier (5K errors + 50 replays) fits early commercial use. Among paid tiers at this volume, AWS CloudWatch RUM (~$5/month) and Sematext Basic ($9/month) are cheapest. Raygun Basic is $80/month billed annually for a flat 100K-session allowance; Atatus Startup is $49/month.

Recommended pick by need

  • Just need Core Web Vitals: Cloudflare Web Analytics, $0.
  • Hobby / pre-launch with light replay: Sentry free.
  • Flat-rate commercial with no replay: Raygun Basic, $80/month billed annually (100K sessions included).
  • Bundled APM + RUM: Atatus Startup, $49/mo.
  • Replay-heavy debugging: LogRocket Core, $69/mo.
  • Performance-budgets workflow: SpeedCurve Starter, $90/mo.
