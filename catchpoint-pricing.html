The pricing model

Quote-only since the LogicMonitor acquisition (December 2025). catchpoint.com/pricing now redirects to logicmonitor.com/pricing, which says Real User Monitoring with Session Replay, formerly sold under Catchpoint, is purchased separately from LogicMonitor's platform packages. No RUM rate is published.

What changed: Catchpoint is now part of LogicMonitor

LogicMonitor's pricing page says its Digital Experience capabilities (Synthetics and Internet Sonar, Real User Monitoring with Session Replay, and Endpoint Monitoring) are the capabilities formerly sold under Catchpoint, which LogicMonitor acquired in December 2025. Synthetics and Internet Sonar are included in LogicMonitor's platform packages; RUM is purchased separately, and LogicMonitor asks buyers to contact it for pricing.

Existing Catchpoint contracts stay in effect for their current term, according to the same page; the platform-package options are reviewed at renewal.

The last published Catchpoint list (August 2026)

The last capture of catchpoint.com/pricing in the Internet Archive, dated 10 August 2026, shows a Quick Start package that “starts at $10,000/yr” and an Expert plan that “starts at $11,988/year and includes Real User Monitoring (RUM)”. Both were starting prices, not a full rate card, and neither is offered on the current page. Treat them as history, useful only as a reference point when you compare a LogicMonitor quote.

How to compare a quote

Because no RUM rate is published, ask the account team to express the quote per 1,000 sessions (or per 1,000 page views) at your traffic, and to say whether session replay is metered separately. That puts it on the same axis as the vendors that do publish:

  • Datadog RUM: $0.15 per 1,000 sessions (Measure) plus $3.00 per 1,000 investigated sessions, annual; Session Replay $2.50 per 1,000.
  • Dynatrace RUM: $2.25 per 1,000 sessions, plus $2.25 per 1,000 for session replay.
  • AWS CloudWatch RUM: $1 per 100,000 data events.

When a Catchpoint (LogicMonitor) quote is worth getting

  • You need synthetic monitoring and RUM from one vendor and already run, or are evaluating, LogicMonitor for infrastructure monitoring.
  • You are an existing Catchpoint customer approaching renewal.
