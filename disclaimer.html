Domain disambiguation

The domain name rumcost.com may, at a casual glance, suggest content about the alcoholic spirit rum. This site does not cover rum the beverage. RUM in the context of this site is the acronym for Real User Monitoring, a category of frontend observability software. The disambiguation banner at the top of every page makes this distinction explicit. If you arrived here looking for the price of a bottle of rum the drink, this is not the right site.
