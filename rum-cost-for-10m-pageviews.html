RUM Cost for 10M Pageviews/Month 2026

At 10 million monthly page views (roughly 5 million sessions), per-session pricing produces eye-watering list bills. AWS CloudWatch RUM stays cheap (~$500/mo); Datadog hits ~$15,750/mo at list. Negotiation framework matters here.

Direct answer
What's RUM cost at 10 million pageviews per month?
AWS CloudWatch RUM ~$500/mo. Sematext Standard tier scales above $200/mo. Atatus Pro $399/mo (5M pageviews, needs Enterprise tier above). Datadog ~$22,500/mo list ($11K-17K negotiated). Dynatrace ~$11,250/mo list. New Relic ~$80/mo (if you're already running broader observability).

At enterprise scale, negotiate

Datadog and Dynatrace published list rates produce $135K-$189K annual bills at this volume. Real enterprise contracts typically close 25-50% below list for committed-spend deals. Effective Datadog cost at 5M sessions is often $95K-$140K/yr rather than $189K/yr. See RUM for enterprise for the full negotiation framework.

Why AWS CloudWatch RUM wins at scale

AWS's per-event pricing model is fundamentally cheaper at scale than per-session. At 5M sessions producing 50M events at $1/100K, AWS is $500/month, about 30x cheaper than Datadog list. The trade-off is UX, integrations, and observability ecosystem maturity. For teams already on AWS infrastructure with comfort in CloudWatch dashboards, AWS CloudWatch RUM is genuinely competitive at enterprise scale.
