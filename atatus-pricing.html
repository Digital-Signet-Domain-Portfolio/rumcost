The pricing model

As of June 2026 Atatus moved to custom pricing, no published tier rates. Marketing emphasises 'no user-based pricing, no complex pricing tiers, no misconfiguration penalties'. 14-day free trial available.

From tier pricing to custom quote

Prior to 2026 Atatus published a tier ladder (Startup, Growth, Business, Pro) priced by page views per month. The 2026 pricing page no longer shows those tiers; it now describes pricing as request-volume-based and prompts visitors to 'Schedule a Call' for a tailored quote.

Atatus marketing emphasises 'no user-based pricing, no complex pricing tiers, no misconfiguration penalties'. The intent is operationally friendly (no surprise per-user fees, no cliff edges between tiers), but the trade-off is procurement opacity, you can't budget Atatus without a sales conversation.

What we know about Atatus as a product

  • Bundled observability suite: APM + Browser Monitoring (RUM) + Logs Monitoring in one product.
  • RUM is included at every tier (no separate per-session meter visible).
  • Session replay availability is not disclosed on the current page.
  • 14-day free trial available for evaluation.

The competitor-blog caveat

Atatus also publishes comparison content on its own blog ('Atatus vs Datadog vs New Relic pricing') that ranks well in SERPs for cross-vendor pricing queries. Read those comparisons with the understanding that Atatus is the vendor; their cost modelling tends to favour Atatus. Cross-check any Atatus blog claim against the original vendor pricing pages.

How to evaluate Atatus when prices aren't public

  • Use the 14-day free trial to understand the product first, before engaging sales.
  • Anchor your quote request against published competitors at your traffic volume, Raygun ($80/mo Basic, billed annually), Sentry ($26/mo Team), Sematext ($9/mo Basic).
  • Ask for the request-volume tier structure in writing, even if final pricing is custom.
  • Request multi-year quote alongside annual, multi-year typically prices 5-15% below annual.

How Atatus compares to alternatives

  • Versus Datadog, Atatus is positioned as an APM + RUM + Logs unified observability platform at mid-market price. Datadog is the enterprise leader at significantly higher cost.
  • Versus Sentry, Sentry has stronger SDK ecosystem and developer ergonomics. Atatus offers broader APM + Logs integration.
  • Versus Raygun, both target mid-market with bundled monitoring. Raygun publishes Basic at $80/mo (billed annually); Atatus does not publish.

When Atatus is worth a sales call

  • You want APM + RUM + Logs unified in one product (rather than vendor-pure RUM).
  • You can budget a custom-quote procurement cycle (typically 1-2 weeks).
  • You're already in conversation with multiple observability vendors and want a mid-market alternative to Datadog.

When to skip Atatus

  • You need transparent published pricing for procurement compliance.
  • You operate at small commercial scale where published cheaper alternatives (Raygun, Sentry, Sematext) are sufficient.
  • You only need RUM, not the bundled APM+Logs suite.
