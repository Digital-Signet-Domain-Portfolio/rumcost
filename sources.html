What we did not cite

We did not aggregate from G2, TrustRadius, Capterra, Software Advice, or any other review-aggregator site. User reviews are useful for product fit assessment but they do not contain current per-session pricing in a structured form. We did not cite vendor case studies or whitepapers, these are marketing collateral, not pricing sources. We did not cite Reddit, Hacker News, or other forum threads, anecdotal pricing reports are unverifiable. We did not cite analyst reports (Gartner, Forrester), these are paywalled and we cannot link to them as sources.
