RUM Cost for 1M Pageviews/Month 2026

At 1 million monthly page views (roughly 500,000 sessions), most free tiers are exhausted. Per-session pricing differences become operationally material, the cheapest viable commercial RUM is around $50/mo, the most expensive is ~$1,575/mo (Datadog RUM, before its replay add-on).

Direct answer
What's RUM cost at 1 million pageviews per month?
AWS CloudWatch RUM ~$50/mo (cheapest if on AWS). Atatus Growth $99/mo. Sentry Team + overage ~$300-500/mo. LogRocket Team $295/mo. Raygun Business ~$800/mo (its 200K-session Team tier is exceeded at 500K sessions). Dynatrace ~$1,125/mo. Datadog ~$2,250/mo. The 45x gap from AWS to Datadog is the dominant cost decision.

The 30x cost spread

AWS CloudWatch RUM at ~$50/month vs Datadog RUM at ~$1,575/month is roughly a 30x spread on the same workload. The difference is mostly product polish (Datadog has best-in-class dashboards, integrations, AIOps; AWS has utility-grade dashboards) and partly distribution model (Datadog is per-session billed with a separate replay add-on; AWS is per-event billed on a single meter).
